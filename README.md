# Wasted-Time
A silly project to keep us from writing on our thesis. 😉 

The project came to fruition on a especially slow day while we were trying to write our thesis. 
It started as a simple collaboration on Syncfiddle, which led to creating a counter for how much time we spent procrastinating.

The idea expanded to add some gifs to make it more interesting. In the current state the gif bounces around the window and can be dragged and dropped around in the window. 

We will surely procrastinate even more so keep an eye out for even more features in the future.

Click [here](https://wastedtime.jacobduvander.se/) to see the project in action.

## What it does

- Counts the time you waste and remembers it between visits.
- A gif bounces around the window. Drag it, throw it (harder flick = faster throw), squeeze it against the edges.
- The buttons run away from your mouse. Throw the gif through them and they scatter.
- The longer you stay, the worse it gets: decoy buttons at 3 minutes, wobbling text at 5, rage gifs at 10, a second gif at 15.
- "Decrease your wasted time" asks if you really want to work, sometimes several times. Say no and it costs you time.
- Go idle for 30 seconds and the page shouts at you.
- Gravity mode (button or `G`), plus an optional sound drone that gets angrier over time.
- A thesis progress bar that starts at 3% and goes backwards while you procrastinate.
- "Share my shame" copies your wasted time to the clipboard.

Animations respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Sound is off by default.
